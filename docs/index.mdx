title: Start here
description: Install Thumbly on your Mac, connect your iPhone and try your first conversation with an AI.
---

Thumbly is a Mac app that lets an AI assistant see and control your iPhone. With your permission, the AI can take screenshots, tap, type and press the phone's buttons. You choose which AI can connect and can pause phone control at any time from the Thumbly app on your Mac.

You need a Mac with macOS 14 or later, an iPhone with iOS 17.4 or later, and a USB data cable. See [Requirements](/docs/install/requirements).

## 1. Install Thumbly

[Install Thumbly](/docs/install/install-thumbly) with one command in Terminal. The service runs in the background and the Thumbly app opens on your Mac.

## 2. Connect your iPhone

[Plug in your iPhone and tap Trust](/docs/connect-your-iphone/plug-in-and-trust), then [turn on Developer Mode](/docs/connect-your-iphone/turn-on-developer-mode). After the first USB pairing you can also [use Wi-Fi](/docs/connect-your-iphone/use-wi-fi-after-pairing).

## 3. Connect your AI

[Connect Claude Code](/docs/connect-your-ai/claude-code), [Claude Desktop](/docs/connect-your-ai/claude-desktop) or [another MCP client](/docs/connect-your-ai/other-mcp-clients), then approve it in the Thumbly app.

## After your first conversation

Add [remote access](/docs/remote-access) if you want Claude.ai or ChatGPT to reach your iPhone. If you get stuck, visit [Help](/docs/troubleshoot/troubleshooting-by-symptom).
