---
title: Claude Desktop
description: Connect the Claude desktop app on your Mac to Thumbly.
---

1. In Claude Desktop, open the settings for connectors (custom MCP servers) and add a server with the URL:

   ```
   http://127.0.0.1:8771/mcp
   ```

2. Connect it. A browser page opens that says **Approve in the Thumbly app**.
3. Click **Open Thumbly**. In the Thumbly app, under **Access**, choose **Allow for 24 hours**, **30 days** or **Until I remove it**.
4. Ask Claude: *Take a screenshot of my iPhone and describe what you see.*
