---
title: Approve and control access
description: Choose which AI can use your iPhone and stop access when you need to.
---

You stay in control of which AI can use your iPhone. Everything happens in the Thumbly app on your Mac. When a client asks for access, you get a notification and the menu bar icon shows the number of pending requests.

- **Approve a request:** open **Access**, check the client's name (and code, for scripts) and choose **Allow for 24 hours**, **30 days** or **Until I remove it**. Choose **Deny** to refuse it. The **Open Thumbly** button on the client's sign-in page takes you straight to the request.
- **Pause phone control:** press **Pause AI control** on the **Status** screen or in the menu bar to stop all AI actions at once. Pending input is cancelled.
- **Remove an AI's access:** open **Access** and press **Remove** next to the client. It must ask again to reconnect.
- **See what happened:** open **Activity** for a log of actions. Activity is kept for 30 days. Screenshots and typed or sent text are never stored.

Approval grants screen and input access together. Only one client controls the phone at a time; control lapses after 90 seconds without activity.
