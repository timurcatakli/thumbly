---
title: Operating limits
description: What Thumbly can and cannot do on your iPhone.
---

- Portrait orientation only; turn on portrait orientation lock.
- Taps, long presses and single-finger drags. No multitouch.
- Typing: printable U.S. ASCII, up to 200 characters.
- Hardware buttons: home, lock, volume up and down, mute, Siri.
- Control lapses after 90 seconds without activity, and another client can then take it.
- An action interrupted before Thumbly knows its result is reported as `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`. It is never replayed automatically: check the screen and choose a new action.
- Screenshots and typed or sent text are never stored in the activity log. Activity is kept for 30 days.
- The phone shows its screen-recording indicator while input is possible.
