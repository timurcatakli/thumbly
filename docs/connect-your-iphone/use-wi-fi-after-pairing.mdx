---
title: Use Wi-Fi after pairing
description: Control your iPhone without the cable once it has been paired over USB.
---

Pairing over USB also creates a Wi-Fi pairing record. After that, Thumbly can reach the iPhone over Wi-Fi when both are on the same network.

1. Finish [Plug in and tap Trust](/docs/connect-your-iphone/plug-in-and-trust) and [Turn on Developer Mode](/docs/connect-your-iphone/turn-on-developer-mode) with the cable first.
2. Make sure the Mac and the iPhone are on the same Wi-Fi network.
3. Unplug the cable. Thumbly reconnects over Wi-Fi; unlocking the iPhone helps it find the phone faster.

USB and Wi-Fi are interchangeable: the same AI tools work over both. If the Thumbly app shows **Wi-Fi pairing needed** (`WIFI_PAIRING_REQUIRED`), plug the iPhone in once more and tap **Trust**.

After an iPhone restart, Thumbly mounts the developer disk image again. This needs internet access on the Mac.
