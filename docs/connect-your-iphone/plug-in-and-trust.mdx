---
title: Plug in and tap Trust
description: Connect your iPhone to your Mac with a cable and approve the connection.
---

Keep the Thumbly app open on your Mac (run `thumbly open` if it is closed). Its setup screen moves forward as each step finishes.

1. Turn on portrait orientation lock in Control Center on the iPhone so the screen stays upright.
2. Connect the iPhone to your Mac with a USB data cable.
3. Unlock the iPhone. When **Trust This Computer?** appears, tap **Trust** and enter your passcode.
4. Keep the cable connected. Thumbly binds this iPhone automatically and the app moves on to Developer Mode.

This step is called pairing. It lets Thumbly recognize your iPhone and also prepares it to connect over Wi-Fi later. Thumbly controls only the iPhone it is bound to; to switch phones, use **Settings → Use another iPhone** in the Thumbly app.

If you tapped **Don't Trust**, unplug the cable, plug it back in and tap **Trust** when the prompt returns. If the phone is not found, try a cable that supports data transfer.
