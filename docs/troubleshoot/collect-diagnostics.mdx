---
title: Collect diagnostics
description: What to send when asking for help.
---

1. In the Thumbly app, open **Settings → Export diagnostics** and save the report. Identifiers are redacted.
2. Note the service state:

   ```sh
   thumbly agent status
   ```

3. Copy the lines around the problem from `~/Library/Logs/Thumbly/thumbly.log`.
4. Open a [bug report](https://github.com/timurcatakli/thumbly/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) and attach what you collected, with your macOS, iPhone and iOS versions.

Remove private data before you share anything. Do not post credentials, your iPhone's UDID or serial number, or screenshots of private content. Report security problems privately as described in the [security policy](https://github.com/timurcatakli/thumbly/security/policy).
